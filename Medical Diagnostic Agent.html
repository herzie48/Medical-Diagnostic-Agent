Naive Bayes posterior (CO5) updated after each answer. Age constraints (CO3) remove impossible diseases first.

The probabilities appear after the first answer.

Care pathway search

Find the cheapest way from Triage to a confirmed diagnosis. Cost is test fee plus waiting cost.

Treatment under uncertainty (game tree)

Doctor (MAX) picks a treatment, nature (MIN) picks the true condition and complications. Change the diagnosis weights to compare worst-case and expected choices.

Safe prescription (constraint satisfaction)

PEAS description

PerformanceCorrect diagnosis, few questions, low test cost, safe prescription, no missed emergency
EnvironmentPatient, hospital pathway, drug formulary
ActuatorsAsk, order tests, prescribe, escalate
SensorsYes/no answers, age, allergies, history, household

Environment: partially observable, single agent, stochastic, sequential, semi-dynamic, discrete. The agent is goal-based and utility-based with a learning component.

Data and probabilities are synthetic and illustrative. Do not use this for real health decisions.
